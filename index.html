General rules

Eligibility: LKG to Class 12, open to all District Matriculation Schools.

Registration: Through the respective School Coordinator. Closes 12 September 2026.

Fee: ₹75 per student, per competition entered.

Participation: Every registered student receives a participation certificate and a medal. Additional 1st / 2nd / 3rd prizes are awarded competition-wise / category-wise.

Students Art Festival: Hazardous materials are not allowed, except knife/blade use for Vegetable Carving, which is permitted under organiser supervision.

Venue: OSTREA Awards, 23 Periyamannara Street, London School Campus, Eral – 628801, Thoothukudi District.

Judges & final decision authority: To be announced. Coming soon

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate?

Students from LKG to Class 12, from any District Matriculation School, are welcome to take part.

What is the registration fee?

₹75 per student, per competition entered.

When does registration close?

Registration closes on 12 September 2026.

When is the final competition?

The final competition is on 16 October 2026.

What competitions are available?

Painting, Drawing, Handwriting, Speech, Solo Dance, and Students Art Festival.

What are the Students Art Festival categories?

Vegetable Carving, Fireless Cooking, Clay Modeling, Recycled Art, and T-Shirt Painting.

What does every registered student receive?

A participation certificate and a medal.

How do I apply?

Registration is through your respective School Coordinator. You can also scan the official QR code or tap "Apply Online" to fill in the student application on this page.

What if my class is not eligible for a competition?

The application will disable competitions that don't match the selected class. Please choose another competition. For anything else, please contact the Organisers.

What time should students report on competition day?

Student reporting time is 9:00 AM, with registration / check-in at 9:30 AM. The school programme runs first, followed by the OSTREA Awards competitions. The exact competition starting time will be announced soon.

When will results be announced?

Results will be announced on the same day. The exact timing is not yet confirmed.

What prizes are given?

Every registered student receives a participation certificate and a medal. 1st, 2nd, and 3rd prizes are also awarded competition-wise / category-wise; exact prize details will be announced soon.
